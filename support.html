Common questions

How do I move my army? Select soldiers (tap Select, then drag a box, or double-tap one soldier to select the whole army) and steer with the thumb stick at the bottom right, or tap the map where they should go.

How do I build? Select villagers, tap the hammer, tap a glowing building button, then tap the map.

Can I replay the tutorial? Yes: pause the game and choose Replay tutorial.

Is my progress saved? Yes, on your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

Does it need an internet connection? No. The whole game runs offline.
