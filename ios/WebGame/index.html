POCKETEMPIRES

a tiny Age-of-Empires–style skirmish

Stick: move the selected units; drag the map to pan the camera
Tap: select · command · gather · attack   Double-tap: all units of that type
Drag the map: pan around   Pinch: zoom
Command button: sword attacks; with villagers selected it builds or resumes their task
Market: trade gathered resources to rebalance your economy · the top HUD shows idle villagers
Goal: gather, build, raise an army… and raze the red Town Center

Best in landscape · In Safari: Share → “Add to Home Screen” for full-screen play
