Modification notice
-------------------

The source meshes and textures have been MODIFIED. Each sprite is a derivative
work produced by rendering a COLLADA mesh from a fixed isometric camera and
sampling its texture map per triangle, with lighting and colour adjustment
applied. No sprite is an unmodified copy of a source file.

Because the originals are CC BY-SA 3.0, these derivatives are distributed under

Regeneration
------------

    python3 scripts/bake_zeroad_art.py

Requires Pillow and the assimp CLI. The output is committed, so no build step
depends on either tool. This is not Release 28, not an official 0 A.D. mobile
edition, and not affiliated with or endorsed by Wildfire Games.
