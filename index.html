The game

A whole RTS in your pocket

Classic village-to-army strategy, rebuilt for a phone screen: no tiny buttons, no box-dragging marathons.

I

Build a living village

Villagers chop, farm, mine and haul. Houses, farms, mills, markets and barracks go up in two taps, and your economy hums while you plan the war.

II

March under your banner

Men-at-arms and archers fight company by company under their standard. A thumb stick steers the whole army, and a double-tap picks every soldier of a kind.

III

Hold the gate

Raise palisades and stone walls, set a gate, garrison your towers and make them pay for every step.

IV

Take their Town Center

Ten levels, from The Quiet Border to No Mercy, against an AI that builds its own village and comes for yours. Burn its Town Center to win.
